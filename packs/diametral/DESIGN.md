## Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Minimal, enduring, elegant. Flat and sharp: 1px rules, no shadows, no rounded corners. Ink on white content slides, punctuated by coloured signature washes. Deliberately away from the colour codes of tech and consulting: refined and structured. The thin grid rules dividing the canvas are part of the language.

## Typography Rules
- **Display:** Ufficio 300 (weight 300), fallback Georgia
- **Body:** Geist (weight 400), fallback Helvetica
- **Label:** Geist Light (weight 300), fallback Helvetica
- Scale: cover_title 52pt · divider_title 50pt · title 23pt · body 12pt · label 12pt · caption 9pt · numeral 60pt · numeral_xl 106pt
- Replace text run by run so each run keeps its font and size.

## Component Stylings
- Strokes: hairline 0.75pt, link 1.25pt, emphasis 1.5pt.
- **charts:** Native and editable. Primary series in the accent, the one element the slide is about in the highlight, further series on the quiet series ramp. Body font 9–10 pt in ink, hairline axes in the rule colour, value gridlines only. No 3D, no shadow, no chart title (the verdict is the slide title), doughnut over pie, direct labels, legend at the bottom only for 2+ series.
- **diagrams:** Native shapes on the grid. Boxes are surface + hairline rule; washes mark an emphasis or foundation tier. The highlight appears as a stroke, numeral, icon or arrow on the active element only, never as a box fill.
- **icons:** Phosphor set, tinted with one brand colour per surface, line marks of at most ~0.5 in.
- **grid:** The visible grid may materialise a few axes (3 at most): it structures, it never decorates.
- **eyebrows:** All-caps label grammar "0X · SECTION / SUBJECT", one form per deck.

## Layout Principles
- Canvas 10 × 5.62 in. Margin 0.2 in.
- Columns: 3-up at x = 0.2 / 3.41 / 6.63 in; 4-up at x = 0.2 / 2.61 / 5 / 7.43 in.
- Title box L0.36 T0.18 W6.63 H0.85, one line of at most 48 characters.
- Body band from T1.5 in; nothing below the footer line at T5.1 in.

## Depth & Elevation
Flat: no shadows, no 3D, no gradients unless the template carries them.

## Do's and Don'ts
- ✅ Clone the signature slides (cover, summary, dividers, closing) once per deck; draw content slides bespoke.
- ✅ Rotate washes so the deck reads as light content punctuated by coloured signatures.
- ✅ Use photography generously, full-bleed panels and image-backed cards, as the exemplar does.
- ✅ Write nominal, verdict-like titles; short labels take no final period.
- ❌ Wash every slide, or use an accent as a background fill on a content slide.
- ❌ Use the status colours as body text or as a second decorative accent.
- ❌ Add a short accent bar above or under a text block (a generated-deck marker, not a motif).
- ❌ Translate the wordmark or the English closing line unless asked.

## Canvas
Slides, not screens: a fixed 10 × 5.62 in canvas. Text never shrinks
to fit; tighten the copy before enlarging a box.

## Agent Prompt Guide
- Use the roles above; never invent a hex value or a font.
- Unknown value: write `[À COMPLÉTER]` (fr), `[TO COMPLETE]` (en), `[POR COMPLETAR]` (es), `[A COMPLETAR]` (pt), in the deck's language.
- Read voice.md before writing copy and exemplar.md before composing.
- Select shapes by shape_id; the template's shape names are unreliable.
